About

Nullnetwork Diagnostics . Runs beside FiveM and saves a report when the game crashes. A report contains the crash dump FiveM wrote (a snapshot of the game's memory), the game log, and basic system facts such as your Windows version, CPU and RAM. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it, or you turned on "Always share".
